# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AeroDNS welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with useful context.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's information.
- Prioritize the safety and well-being of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.

For sensitive reports, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and public spaces where someone is representing AeroDNS.
